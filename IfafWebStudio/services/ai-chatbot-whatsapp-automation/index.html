FAQ

Common questions, answered directly.

Straight answers about the service, scope and how projects work.

Does every business need an AI chatbot?

No. AI and automation help when there are repeated questions or tasks—room availability, menus, prices, routes or service details. If a simple WhatsApp button solves the problem, that is the better option.

What can WhatsApp automation do for a small business?

It can send instant replies to common questions, collect enquiry details in a structured way and route customers to the right person or information, so fewer messages go unanswered.

Can automation work with my existing website?

In most cases, yes. The approach depends on how the current website is built; it is assessed before recommending a solution.

Is AI automation suitable for hotels, restaurants and taxi services?

Yes. These businesses receive many repeated enquiries about availability, menus, prices and routes, which makes them good candidates. See hotel, restaurant and taxi websites.
