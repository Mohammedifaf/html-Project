Definitions

What are GEO and AEO?

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the practice of making a business easy for AI search tools—such as ChatGPT search, Google AI Overviews, Gemini and Perplexity—to understand and cite. Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) structures content as direct answers to specific questions so it can appear in featured snippets, voice answers and AI responses.

Both build on classic SEO: accurate entity information, structured data, clear page structure, crawlable content and consistent details across the web.

FAQ

Common questions, answered directly.

Straight answers about the service, scope and how projects work.

What is local SEO?

Local SEO improves how a business appears in location-based searches such as “website developer in Manipal” or “resort near Udupi”, including Google Maps results. It relies on a well-optimized Google Business Profile, consistent business details and relevant local content.

What is GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)?

GEO is the practice of making a business easy for AI search tools such as ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Gemini and Perplexity to understand and cite, using clear facts, structured data, crawlable content and consistent information across the web.

What is AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)?

AEO structures website content as clear, direct answers to the questions customers ask, so it can be used in featured snippets, voice assistants and AI-generated answers.

Can you guarantee first-page rankings on Google?

No. Nobody can honestly guarantee rankings. IfafWebStudio improves the factors that are under your control—technical health, content, structured data and business profiles—which improves the chances of being found.

How long does SEO take to show results?

Technical fixes and Google Business Profile improvements can help within weeks, while content-led ranking growth usually builds over a few months depending on competition and location.

Do you optimize Google Business Profiles in Manipal and Udupi?

Yes. Google Business Profile optimization includes categories, services, descriptions, photos and consistent contact details, alongside local SEO on the website.
