FAQ

Common questions, answered directly.

Straight answers about the service, scope and how projects work.

What is a QR digital menu?

A QR digital menu is a web page with your menu that diners open by scanning a QR code at the table or counter. It works on any phone camera without an app, and the menu can be updated without reprinting.

Can the menu be updated after launch?

Yes. The menu is set up so it can be updated when dishes or prices change; whether you edit it yourself or have updates managed is decided during the project.

Do you help restaurants with Google Business Profile and social media?

Yes. Google Business Profile optimization, local SEO, social media management, AI video and food photography/videography support are available.

Do you work with restaurants and cafes in Udupi?

Yes. The studio is based in Manipal and works with restaurants, cafes and food businesses in Udupi and other locations.

How much does a restaurant website cost?

Cost depends on the number of pages, features and content involved. The enquiry form lists budget ranges starting from ₹20,000–₹30,000, and you can choose “Need guidance” if you are unsure. Share your requirement for a specific quote.
