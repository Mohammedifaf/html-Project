FAQ

Common questions, answered directly.

Straight answers about the service, scope and how projects work.

Should I choose WordPress or a custom website?

Choose WordPress if you want to edit content yourself through a dashboard; choose a custom-coded site if you want maximum speed and a tightly controlled design with fewer moving parts. The recommendation depends on your business and is made before the build.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. Website redesigns keep what already works and improve structure, design, mobile experience, speed and SEO foundations.

Will my website work well on mobile phones?

Yes. Every website is responsive and designed mobile-first, with call and WhatsApp actions easy to reach.

Is SEO included in website development?

On-page SEO foundations—titles, descriptions, headings, structured data, sitemaps and speed—are considered during the build. Ongoing local SEO and GEO is available as a separate service.

How much does a business website cost?

Cost depends on the number of pages, features and content involved. The enquiry form lists budget ranges starting from ₹20,000–₹30,000, and you can choose “Need guidance” if you are unsure. Share your requirement for a specific quote.
