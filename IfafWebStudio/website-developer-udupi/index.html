Is there a website developer near Udupi?

Yes. IfafWebStudio is based in Manipal, a few kilometres from Udupi city in the same district, and builds websites for Udupi businesses including hotels, resorts, restaurants and taxi operators.

Which Udupi-area websites has IfafWebStudio built?

Udupi-area work includes Island Resort (islandresort.in) and East West Travels in Manipal (eastwesttravelsmanipal.com). Other live projects include AMB Guest House, Golden Riveria and EazyRide Cab.

Can you help my Udupi business show up on Google Maps?

Yes. Google Business Profile optimization and local SEO help Udupi businesses appear for searches such as “resort near Udupi” or “taxi in Udupi”.

Do you also work with businesses in Mangaluru, Kundapura or elsewhere?

Yes. Most work is coordinated over WhatsApp, phone and email, so the studio can work with businesses across Karnataka and India.
